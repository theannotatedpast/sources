# Sources: Why Did the Vasa Sink? What the 1628 Inquest Actually Says

*The Annotated Past* – History, told properly – every claim sourced, every source listed.

On Sunday 10 August 1628, the new Swedish warship Vasa sank in Stockholm harbour on its maiden voyage, after sailing only about 1,300 metres. Popular stories blame the king, a drunk crew or too little ballast; this video sets them against the surviving records of the 1628 investigation, where the builders said the king approved the ship's dimensions, every witness in that record who was asked about the guns said they were secured, and the officers blamed the ship's instability. The surviving inquest record ends without a verdict, and whether the king changed the design midway is still disputed.
