# Sources: Stockholm Bloodbath 1520: The King's Story vs Sweden's Propaganda

*The Annotated Past* – History, told properly – every claim sourced, every source listed.

On 4 November 1520 Christian II was crowned king of Sweden in Stockholm, and within days supporters of the dead regent Sten Sture were executed without regard to his sealed promise of amnesty. We set the accounts from the king's side, to Swedish subjects and to the Pope, beside Gustav Vasa's 1524 propaganda woodcut (lost, and known from a 1676 copperplate) and Olaus Petri's chronicle. Then we follow the historians' dispute over the blame: Lauritz Weibull put the weight on Archbishop Gustav Trolle, while Sverre Bagge finds it more reasonable that Christian staged the bloodbath himself.
