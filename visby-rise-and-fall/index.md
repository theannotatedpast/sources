# Sources: Visby: The Rise and Fall of a Medieval Baltic Trading Town

*The Annotated Past* – History, told properly – every claim sourced, every source listed.

Outside Visby's town wall stands a memorial cross for the Gutes who fell before its gates in 1361, and an estimated 1,800 dead lie in the mass graves around it. Follow Visby from Gotland's trading farmers to its time as one of the leading trading towns of medieval Europe, and on through a decline that the sources date differently. At the turning point, we set what the chronicles of the time record, a great levy in gold and silver, beside the popular stories of barrels of silver and a gate shut on the peasants.
