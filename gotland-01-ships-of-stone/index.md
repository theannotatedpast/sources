# Sources: Gotland, Episode 1: Ships of Stone

*The Annotated Past* – History, told properly – every claim sourced, every source listed.

On Gotland, an island in the Baltic Sea, people once raised ships of standing stones, mainly along the coasts, and centuries later people on the island were carving ships into memorial stones. In this first episode of the series we follow that image from the Mesolithic seal hunters who first reached the island, through the ship settings traditionally dated to about 1100 to 500 BC, to the picture stones made from about AD 400 to about 1100, on which the ship is the most frequent motif. Along the way we set out what the stones can and cannot tell us: the ship settings were traditionally read as graves, yet by Joakim Wehlin's count 14 of the 54 properly reported excavations on Gotland found no burial, and Wehlin argues that some may have been meeting places for seafarers.
