# Sources: Gotland, Episode 2: The Gutasaga

*The Annotated Past* – History, told properly – every claim sourced, every source listed.

Gotland's own legend, Guta saga, says the island once sank by day and rose at night, that after a man named Tjelvar first brought fire it never sank again, and that the descendants of three brothers later grew so many that every third person was chosen by lot and sent away. In this second episode of the series we tell the saga in its own order, from the one manuscript of about 1350 in which it survives in Gutnish, and then set it beside the evidence: the division of the island into thirds is first recorded in 1213. Whether the exodus remembers anything real is still argued: in 1943 Lauritz Weibull called it a learned retelling of an ancient tale told of many peoples, while the saga's English editor finds it probable that some genuine oral material about Gotland lies behind it.
