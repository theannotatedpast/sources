# Sources: Gotland, Episode 3: Silver Island

*The Annotated Past* – History, told properly – every claim sourced, every source listed.

Gotland is an island in the Baltic Sea that the writers of the Viking Age barely noticed, yet hundreds of Viking-Age hoards of silver and other precious metal have come out of its soil, and more are found every year. In this third episode of the series we follow the silver from the landing places on the coast to the farms inland, and set out why scholars still disagree, both on how many hoards there are and on why they went into the ground: trade, silver hidden at the farm, savings, offerings or emergencies. One 2016 study concludes that no single grand theory explains it, because people then gave silver both economic and symbolic value.
