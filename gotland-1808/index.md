# Sources: When Russia Took Gotland, and How Sweden Got It Back (1808)

*The Annotated Past* – History, told properly – every claim sourced, every source listed.

On the afternoon of 22 April 1808, Russian troops began to land on Gotland, an island with no regular troops, and on 18 May they sailed away after laying down their weapons at Slite. There was no battle for Gotland in 1808. Using the tsar's written instruction to Rear Admiral Bodisco, the Swedish officials' reports and the articles of capitulation, this video shows how the island was taken and handed back, and where the Swedish reports disagree about how the occupiers behaved and what the occupation cost.
