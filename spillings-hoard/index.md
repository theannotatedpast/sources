# Sources: The Spillings Hoard: How Arab Coins Ended Up Under a Gotland Floor

*The Annotated Past* – History, told properly – every claim sourced, every source listed.

In 1999, silver began to come out of the ground on a farm called Spillings on Gotland: about 14,000 coins, hidden under the floor of a Viking Age building. Most were Arabic dirhams, struck at dozens of mints in the central lands of the Arab Caliphate. We follow the coins north and show what they can tell us, including that the hoard went into the ground no earlier than 870/871, and what they cannot: who hid the silver, and whether the north paid for it mainly in furs or in captives, which by Jankowiak's account is still an open debate.
