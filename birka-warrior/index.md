# Sources: Was the Birka Warrior Really a Woman? What the Sources Show

*The Annotated Past* – History, told properly – every claim sourced, every source listed.

A chamber grave at Birka, surrounded by weapons and with 2 horses at its edge, was assumed to hold a man from its excavation until a 2017 genomic study found the sampled bones biologically female. This video reads that paper, Fedir Androshchuk's 2018 critique and the team's 2019 reply side by side, texts that do not answer each other. It sorts each question into settled, left open or interpretation: the sex of the bones, whether they belong to this grave, whether a second body lay there, and whether the person buried there was a warrior at all.
