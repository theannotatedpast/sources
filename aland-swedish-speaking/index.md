# Sources: How Swedish-Speaking Åland Ended Up in Finland

*The Annotated Past* – History, told properly – every claim sourced, every source listed.

In February 1918, a deputation from Åland presented the King of Sweden with the islanders' wish to be reunited with Sweden, yet in 1921 the League of Nations decided the dispute in Finland's favour. This video follows the documents behind that outcome, from the 1809 Treaty of Fredrikshamn and the 1856 convention that barred fortifications on the islands to the League's decision and the guarantees for the Swedish language that Finland had to give. At the end of 2024, according to Åland's official statistics, 85.2 per cent of its inhabitants were registered with Swedish as their mother tongue.
